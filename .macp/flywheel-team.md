## Veto Authority

- **Alton (Human Orchestrator):** Absolute veto on all decisions — final human authority.
- **L (CEO, AI-Generated):** Strategic veto under Alton's delegated authority.
- **XV (CIO):** Strategic go/no-go veto on commercial, protocol, and strategic decisions.
- **Z (Guardian):** Ethics veto on any decision with ethical implications.
- **CS (Validator):** Security veto on any decision with security implications.
- **RNA (CSO):** Technical infeasibility/security risk veto on implementation decisions.

## Execution Protocol

1. **Task Receipt:** The orchestrator (Alton or L) defines the task and activation level.
2. **Primary Execution:** The primary agent (T or RNA) executes the task.
3. **Cross-Validation:** Secondary agents validate the output.
4. **Synthesis:** Primary agent synthesizes feedback into final output.
5. **Human Approval:** Alton or L reviews and approves the final output.
6. **Record:** Documentation is updated and handoff records are created in `.macp/`.
